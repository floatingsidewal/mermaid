TrueScale Mode - Proportional Widths

With trueScale, stage widths reflect actual data values proportionally. 

      funnel showData trueScale
        title Revenue by Quarter (Proportional)
        "Q1": 2500000
        "Q2": 1800000
        "Q3": 900000
        "Q4": 450000
